Privacy Policy

Electron Push · Effective [November 11], 2026 · 한국어

Electron Push (“the app”) is an organic chemistry study app provided by [Daum Kim] (“the developer”). The app has no accounts, and the developer does not collect your personal information.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your study progress (completion marks, practice scores) and settings (learn/practice mode, language) are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer or anyone else. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data (or your browser data).

2. Collection and sharing

The app contains no ads, analytics, or sign-in, and does not collect, share, or transfer information about you. The Android and iOS apps work without an internet connection.

The website version is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit it, GitHub may process access logs such as your IP address under its own policy; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

3. Children

The app is intended for university-level learners and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 14 in Korea).

4. Retention and deletion

The developer does not retain personal information. Study records on your device are deleted immediately when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
