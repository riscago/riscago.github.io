Privacy Policy

Electron Push · Effective November 13, 2026 · 한국어

Electron Push (“the app”) is an organic chemistry study app provided by Daum Kim (“the developer”). The app has no accounts, and the developer does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address, or phone number.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your study progress (completion marks, practice scores) and settings (learn/practice mode, language) are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data (or your browser data).

2. Advertising and third parties

The app shows ads so it can stay free. Ads are provided by Google LLC:

  • Android and iOS app: Google AdMob
  • Website: Google AdSense (if used)

To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID, iOS IDFA/IDFV) and app set ID
  • IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location) and device information such as model and operating system
  • Interactions with ads, such as impressions and clicks
  • Diagnostic information such as crash logs

This information is used to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, and is handled under Google's policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Google may process this information in the United States and other countries.

3. Your choices about personalized ads

  • Android: Settings → Google (or Privacy) → Ads lets you reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • iOS: you can decline the “Allow tracking” request on first launch and change it anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • European Economic Area, UK, and other regions that require consent: a consent form appears on first launch; you can change your choice later with “Ad privacy options” at the bottom of the home screen.

If you turn off personalized ads, you will still see ads, but they won't be based on your interests.

4. Children

The app is intended for university-level learners and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 14 in Korea).

5. Retention and deletion

The developer does not retain personal information. Study records on your device are deleted immediately when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Daum Kim at riscago02@gmail.com. You can manage information Google processes for ads through your Google account settings or the choices in section 3.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the change will be posted in the app and on this page.
